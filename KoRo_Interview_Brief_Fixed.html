WHAT IS HAMS

KoRo's internal analytics and attribution measurement system. Connected to their actual order database. HAMS = verified purchases only (real order IDs). Different from Meta/Google platform data which includes modelled iOS conversions and view-through attribution. We don't know what the acronym stands for — it's likely a playful food brand internal name. Good question to ask in the interview: "What does HAMS stand for? I kept seeing it but couldn't find it defined."

1. Fix the Signal — CAPI Server-Side Integration

Now: Meta estimates 65% of iOS purchase events. HAMS verified purchases are not feeding back to Meta. Platform overclaims, algorithm optimises on ghost conversions.

Recommendation: Push HAMS purchase events via GTM server-side container to Meta CAPI: event=Purchase, value=EUR order value, hashed email+phone. Fires even for iOS users who rejected ATT. Meta deduplicates CAPI vs pixel automatically. This is a 2–3 week dev task, highest ROI fix in the entire programme.

Expected impact: Meta algorithm quality improves immediately. Expect nCAC to drop 15–30% within 4–6 weeks as Meta stops wasting budget optimising toward modelled ghost conversions.

Bring to: Sophia (strategic decision) · Sarah/Yilin (implementation brief) · Dev team

2. NewCustomerPurchase Custom Event — Stop Paying for Returners

Now: Meta Reach optimises for all Purchase events — 76% are new customers but the algorithm cannot distinguish. It will take the easiest conversions, which are often warm audiences.

Recommendation: Create a custom CAPI event: NewCustomerPurchase. Logic: if HAMS flags attributed_new > 0, fire this event instead of standard Purchase. Tell Meta Reach to optimise exclusively for this event. Meta bids for first-time buyers only. Same mechanism used at Yara (via Segment CDP) — proven approach.

Expected impact: nCAC improvement expected from EUR 63 toward EUR 45–50 within 4–6 weeks. Spend efficiency improves without needing to cut budget.

Fix: Pull Awin partner breakdown → identify coupon publishers → remove them → real nCAC will be higher but trustworthy.
