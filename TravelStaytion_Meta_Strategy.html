1 Why Meta is the Right Channel
Audience ScaleFacebook has the largest concentration of Airbnb hosts, landlords, vacation rental owners and property investors of any single platform. Millions of property owners are active daily.
Targeting DepthInterest, behavioural, lookalike, and custom audiences allow extremely precise reach to property owners — not just broad demographics. No other social platform matches this granularity for this audience.
Visual FormatsImage and video ads are ideal for property acquisition. A well-photographed property with a clear earning potential message converts at low cost.
No Minimum SpendMeta works at any budget. €50/day gives strong reach and learning — enough data for the algorithm to optimise within 7–14 days.
Full-Funnel ControlMeta can run awareness, consideration and conversion campaigns simultaneously, or in sequence, from a single platform and Ads Manager account.
Pixel & RetargetingOnce the Meta Pixel is installed on the Travel Staytion host registration page, you own a retargeting audience that compounds in value over time — visitors, partial completions, drop-offs.
2 Audience Strategy & Targeting Theory

Interest targeting · Behavioural targeting · Broad + algorithmic expansion

Video viewers · Page engagers · Website visitors (non-host pages)
BOFU — Bottom of Funnel: High-Intent Retargeting  |  Convert property owners who nearly signed up
Host registration page visitors · Partial form completions · Drop-offs
Audience 1 — Interest Targeting (TOFU)

Target people with demonstrated interest in property hosting and rental income:

  • Airbnb hosting & vacation rental management
  • Property investment & real estate
  • Holiday home ownership
  • Rental income & passive income from property
  • Home improvement & property renovation

Cold audience Broad reach Brand awareness

Audience 2 — Behavioural Targeting (TOFU)

Target users whose on-platform behaviour signals property ownership:

  • People who have interacted with Airbnb, Booking.com host or VRBO pages
  • Users who clicked real estate or property listing ads
  • Engaged with property management content
  • Travel enthusiasts who also engage with real estate content (dual signal)

Behavioural Higher intent

Audience 3 — Lookalike Audiences (TOFU/MOFU)

Seed lookalikes from your highest-quality existing data sources:

  • 1% LAL from existing registered hosts — highest priority seed list
  • 1–2% LAL from host registration page visitors
  • 1–3% LAL from email list of past enquiries
  • Test 1% vs 2–3% LAL to balance precision vs scale

Lookalike Algorithm-driven Scalable

Audience 4 — Retargeting: Page & Video Engagers (MOFU)

Re-engage people who interacted with Travel Staytion on Meta but did not visit the site:

  • People who watched 50%+ of a property owner video ad
  • Facebook Page visitors and post engagers (last 90 days)
  • Instagram profile visitors and Story viewers (last 60 days)
  • People who clicked "Learn More" but did not convert

Warm audience Lower CPL

Audience 5 — High-Intent Retargeting (BOFU)

The highest-converting audience. Requires Meta Pixel on host registration page:

  • Host registration page visitors who did not complete sign-up
  • Partial form completions and drop-offs (ViewContent event)
  • People who visited the host page 2+ times (stronger signal)
  • Window: last 30 days for recency; last 60 days as secondary

BOFU Highest intent Pixel required

Audience Exclusions — Critical

Always exclude from all cold audiences:

  • Existing registered hosts (CRM upload)
  • Traveler/guest audiences (interest: booking holidays, travel deals)
  • Recent converters (last 30 days) to avoid wasted spend

8 Advanced Strategies to Test After Month 1
Advantage+ Audience Campaigns
Meta's AI-driven audience mode. You provide minimal audience signals and Meta finds the best converters automatically. Best used once you have 30+ conversions on the Pixel, giving the algorithm real data to work from. Test as a separate campaign against your manual targeting.
Lead Gen Forms (Instant Forms)
Instead of sending traffic to the Travel Staytion website, capture host enquiries directly inside Meta using an Instant Form. Lower friction = higher volume. Trade-off: lead quality may be lower. Test at €10/day alongside conversion campaigns. Pre-fill name and email from Meta profile reduces drop-off.
Seasonal & Event-Based Targeting
Align campaign pushes with moments when property owners are most motivated: post-summer (September — empty calendar anxiety), January (new year, new income resolution), pre-peak season (March–April). Increase daily budget by €10–15 during these windows.
CRM Custom Audiences & Suppression
Upload your full host email list to Meta as a Custom Audience for exclusion (avoid wasting spend on existing hosts) and as a seed for LAL. Upload partial sign-up or enquiry lists as a separate warm audience for retargeting with a re-engagement message. Refresh CRM uploads monthly.
9 Next Steps
1 Install Meta Pixel Today Place the Meta Pixel on the Travel Staytion host registration page. Add ViewContent, Lead, and CompleteRegistration standard events. Verify in Events Manager before launching any campaign. This is the single most important action.
2 Upload CRM List Upload existing host email list to Meta Business Manager as a Custom Audience. Use as exclusion on all cold campaigns immediately. Use as LAL seed for Lookalike Prospecting campaign.
3 Produce Creatives Priority order: (1) Facebook/Instagram Feed Image 1080×1080 with real property — earning headline. (2) 15-second Reel/Story video with property owner hook. (3) 3-card Carousel. Minimum 2 creatives per ad set at launch.
4 Build Campaigns in Ads Manager Set up 3 campaigns as per Section 3. Name ad sets clearly (e.g. "TOFU – Airbnb Interest – Feed Image"). Use Campaign Budget Optimisation (CBO) for prospecting campaigns to let Meta distribute budget to best-performing ad sets automatically.
5 Launch & Enter Learning Phase Launch all 3 campaigns simultaneously. Do not change budgets, audiences or creatives for the first 7 days. Allow the learning phase to complete. Monitor delivery and Pixel events only.
6 30-Day Performance Review After 30 days, review CPL by campaign and ad set. Identify the best-performing audience × creative combination. Shift budget toward it. Drop any combination above €25 CPL. Begin testing Advantage+ and Lead Gen Forms as per Section 8.
