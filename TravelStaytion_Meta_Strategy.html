01

Why Meta is the Right Channel

Audience ScaleFacebook holds the largest concentration of Airbnb hosts, landlords, vacation rental owners and property investors of any single platform. Millions of property owners are active daily.
Targeting DepthInterest, behavioural, lookalike and custom audiences allow extremely precise reach to property owners — not just broad demographics. No other social platform matches this granularity for this audience.
Visual FormatsImage and video ads are ideal for property acquisition. A well-photographed property with a clear earning potential message converts at low cost.
No Minimum SpendMeta works at any budget. €50/day provides strong reach and sufficient data for the algorithm to optimise within 7–14 days.
Full-Funnel ControlMeta runs awareness, consideration and conversion campaigns simultaneously from a single Ads Manager account — no need for multiple platforms at this stage.
Pixel & RetargetingOnce the Meta Pixel is installed on the Travel Staytion host registration page, you own a retargeting audience that compounds in value over time — visitors, partial completions, drop-offs.
02

Audience Strategy & Targeting

Top of Funnel — Cold Audiences  ·  Reach new property owners who don't know Travel Staytion
Interest targeting  ·  Behavioural targeting  ·  Broad + algorithmic expansion
Middle of Funnel — Warm Audiences  ·  Re-engage people who showed intent
Video viewers  ·  Page engagers  ·  Website visitors (non-host pages)
Bottom of Funnel — High-Intent Retargeting  ·  Convert property owners who nearly signed up
Host registration page visitors  ·  Partial form completions  ·  Drop-offs
Audience 1 — Interest Targeting (TOFU)
  • Airbnb hosting & vacation rental management
  • Property investment & real estate
  • Holiday home ownership
  • Rental income & passive income from property
  • Home improvement & property renovation
Cold audience · Broad reach · Brand awareness
Audience 2 — Behavioural Targeting (TOFU)
  • Users who interacted with Airbnb, Booking.com host or VRBO pages
  • Users who clicked real estate or property listing ads
  • Engaged with property management content
  • Travel enthusiasts who also engage with real estate content
Behavioural · Higher intent than interest targeting
Audience 3 — Lookalike Audiences (TOFU/MOFU)
  • 1% LAL from existing registered hosts — highest priority seed
  • 1–2% LAL from host registration page visitors
  • 1–3% LAL from email list of past enquiries
  • Test 1% vs 2–3% to balance precision vs scale
Algorithm-driven · Scalable · Requires seed data
Audience 4 — Page & Video Engagers (MOFU)
  • People who watched 50%+ of a property owner video ad
  • Facebook Page visitors and post engagers (last 90 days)
  • Instagram profile visitors and Story viewers (last 60 days)
  • People who clicked "Learn More" but did not convert
Warm audience · Lower CPL than cold
Audience 5 — High-Intent Retargeting (BOFU)
  • Host registration page visitors who did not complete sign-up
  • Partial form completions and drop-offs (ViewContent event)
  • People who visited the host page 2+ times
  • Window: last 30 days primary · last 60 days secondary
Highest intent · Lowest CPL · Pixel required
Exclusions — Always Apply
  • Existing registered hosts (CRM upload)
  • Traveler/guest audiences (interest: booking holidays)
  • Recent converters (last 30 days)
Apply to all cold and lookalike campaigns

Bidding Strategy

CampaignBidding StrategyRationaleDaily Budget
Cold Prospecting Lowest Cost (auto-bid) Maximises reach within budget during the learning phase. Allow Meta to optimise before constraining it with a cost cap. €25
Lookalike Prospecting Lowest Cost (auto-bid) Same rationale. Once 50+ conversions/week are recorded, consider switching to Cost Cap at your target CPL. €15
Retargeting (BOFU) Highest Value or Cost Cap Audience is small and high-intent. Set Cost Cap at €20 max CPL once benchmark data exists. Prioritise quality over volume. €10
Total Daily €50
Learning Phase: Each ad set needs ~50 optimisation events to exit learning. At €50/day across 3 campaigns and an estimated CPL of €8–15, expect learning to complete within 7–14 days. Do not change budgets, audiences or creatives during this period.

07

30-Day Optimisation Roadmap

PhaseDaysActionsDecision Points
Week 1
Launch		 1–7 Install Pixel. Launch all 3 campaigns. Run 2 creatives per ad set. Use Lowest Cost bidding. Do not touch campaigns. Confirm Pixel is firing correctly on the host registration page via Events Manager. Verify impressions are delivering.
Week 2
Learn		 8–14 Monitor frequency — keep below 2.5 for cold audiences. Check which creatives are driving the most link clicks. Confirm ad sets are exiting learning. If any ad set is stuck in learning, consolidate audiences or increase budget by 20%. Pause creatives with CTR below 0.5%.
Week 3–4
Optimise		 15–30 Pause lowest-performing prospecting ad set. Shift €5/day to retargeting if BOFU audience exceeds 1,000 people. Test 1 new creative variation. If retargeting CPL is below €10, increase its budget. If cold prospecting CPL exceeds €25, test new interest combinations or switch to Advantage+ audience.
