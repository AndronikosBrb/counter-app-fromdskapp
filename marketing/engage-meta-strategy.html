01 · Strategic approach

Two audiences, one disciplined structure

Both briefs share the same job — turn cold, high-intent property professionals into contact-form leads — but speak to two distinct mindsets. We keep the account deliberately simple so a small budget concentrates signal instead of scattering it, and we let the creative do the diversification.

Principle 01

One ad set per campaign

A single, well-defined ad set per campaign — no audience fragmentation. With ≤ €200/month, splitting into multiple ad sets would starve each of the ~50 weekly events Meta needs to learn. One pool, one signal.

Principle 02

Diversify inside the ad set

Five creatives run together in each ad set. Meta's delivery finds the winner faster than we can guess it, and each creative attacks a different motivation — income, trust, convenience, partnership, proof.

Principle 03

Brand-led, not discount-led

These owners choose a partner, not a coupon. Every creative leans on Travelstaytion's real equity — TÜV AUSTRIA-checked quality, Host Reception, a network since 2011 — to earn a high-consideration form fill.

03 · Budget & optimisation

Making €200 work — and the honest minimum

The brief asks for the minimum recommended budget. Here is the straight answer, and the allocation that gets the most out of the €200/month cap.

Monthly allocation (cap €200)

CampaignDailyMonthlyShare
C1 — Owners / Management€3.70€11055%
C2 — Managers / Partners€3.00€9045%
Total€6.70€200100%

C1 gets the slight majority: the "management services" audience is broader and warmer, so it converts the incremental euro more efficiently. Both stay above Meta's practical floor for a single-ad-set conversion campaign.

Phased optimisation event

At €3–4/day a campaign will rarely see enough form submits to exit the learning phase on that event alone. So we optimise in two phases and never sit in "Learning Limited" blind:

  • Phase 1 (Weeks 1–3): optimise for a mid-funnel signal — Landing Page View plus a custom "Form Started" event — to feed the pixel and cut cost-per-click while creatives sort themselves out.
  • Phase 2 (Week 4+): switch the optimisation event to Contact Form Submit (Lead) once the pixel has enough volume, keeping the winning 2–3 creatives live.

Bid strategy: Highest Volume (no cap) during learning, revisit a cost cap only once we have a stable CPA baseline.

!

The honest minimum. €200/month runs a credible test-and-learn across both campaigns and will surface which angles and audiences convert — but it is a learning budget, not a scaling one. To reliably exit the learning phase on form submits in a 4-month window, a working range of €400–€600/month per campaign is the realistic minimum for volume. Recommendation: start at €200 to validate creative and CPA, then scale the winner.

06 · Measurement & optimisation

How we read success

Primary KPI is completed contact forms. Everything else tells us why the number is moving and where to steer budget.

Primary

  • Completed contact forms (conversions)
  • Cost per lead / CPA

Efficiency

  • Unique landing-page visits
  • CPC & CTR
  • Cost per landing-page view

Quality

  • Form completion rate (6/6 = 100%, 5/6 = 83% …)
  • Avg. session duration
  • Creative-level winner vs. dropped
✓

Tracking prerequisites before launch: Meta Pixel + Conversions API on both landing pages; a standard Lead event on form submit and a custom Form Started event on step 1; per-step events to power the completion-rate KPI; UTM tagging on every creative. Without these, the primary KPI can't be measured — this is the one true launch blocker.

07 · Roadmap

From launch to 4-month readout

0

Pre-launch — tracking & assets

Confirm Pixel/CAPI + events, upload CRM exclusion lists, finalise C2 geo list, produce the 10 creatives (5 per campaign). Sign-off on the two landing pages.

1

Weeks 1–3 — learn

Both campaigns live, one ad set each, all 5 creatives running. Optimise for Landing Page View + Form Started. No changes for the first 7 days to protect the learning phase.

2

Week 4 — switch & prune

Move optimisation to Contact Form Submit. Pause the 2 weakest creatives per campaign; keep budget flowing to proven angles. First monthly performance check & optimisation actions.

3

Months 2–4 — optimise

Monthly reviews: refresh tiring creative, refine audiences off winning signals, and — budget permitting — make the case to scale the stronger campaign beyond €200.

4

End of month 4 — final report

Full read on CPA, lead volume & quality, winning angles per audience, and a scaling recommendation for the ongoing programme.
