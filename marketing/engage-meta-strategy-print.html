02

Campaigns: objective, goal, focus

Campaign 1

Owners

Objective
Leads / Conversions. Optimise for contact-form submits.
Goal
Acquire property owners in Greece and Cyprus as property-management clients.
Focus
Educate on the value proposition and drive high-intent traffic to the ENGAGE income page.
Campaign 2

Local partners

Objective
Leads / Conversions. Optimise for contact-form submits.
Goal
Acquire owners and managers in seasonal Greek and Cypriot destinations as local partners.
Focus
Educate on the partner value proposition and drive traffic to the local-partner page.

C1 audience and exclusions

  • Reach: owners of 5–50+ units; Greece and Cyprus; interests in short-term rental, property management, real estate; lookalikes of top owners.
  • Exclude: registered members and past form submitters (CRM lists), plus recent completers via Pixel.

C2 audience and exclusions

  • Reach: managers of 5–50+ units in seasonal destinations; interests in PMS tools (Guesty, Avantio, Hostaway), VRMA and VRWS, STR regulations; lookalikes of top members.
  • Exclude: registered members and past form submitters (CRM lists), plus recent completers via Pixel.

04

UGC remarketing on website visitors

From month two, one remarketing ad set per category shows a UGC video where a real person explains the Travelstaytion USPs. Each targets only the website visitors of its own campaign who did not convert.

  • Owners set (€15/day): a person explains income, hands-off management, TÜV AUSTRIA quality, Host Reception. Targets non-converting visitors of the ENGAGE income page.
  • Partners set (€15/day): a person explains portfolio growth, workflow fit, off-season demand, vetted community. Targets non-converting visitors of the local-partner page.
  • Split by campaign, the Meta way: Meta does not read UTMs for audiences, so build a Website Custom Audience by URL. Method A, by landing page (each campaign uses a different page). Method B, by UTM value (utm_campaign=engage_owners vs engage_partners). Run both.
  • Each ad set: include that campaign's Website Custom Audience, exclude the form-submit (Lead) audience. No crossover between the two videos.
  • Requirements: Pixel and Conversions API on both pages capturing the full URL; retargeting window up to 180 days.
05

Strategy in three sentences

1. Two Meta prospecting campaigns run in parallel at €200 per day, each with one ad set and ten static ads tested in Greek and English.

2. After twenty days the winning ads keep the budget while weaker ones continue at a lower spend, so the account learns fast without wasting spend.

3. From month two, two UGC remarketing sets bring back the website visitors who did not convert, targeted separately by campaign, turning warm traffic into contact-form leads.
